Built to Make Hosting Renewal Math Impossible to Hide

SkyeFlare (skyeflare.com) is an independent web hosting comparison utility, 1-to-3 year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) calculator, and active promotional deal tracker. We do not operate servers or sell hosting plans of our own.

01. How Our Automated Deal-Tracking Pipeline Works

Every 24 hours, an automated GitHub Actions verification job runs scripts/update_deals.py against official public offer pages for Hostinger, Bluehost, Namecheap, SiteGround, A2 Hosting, DreamHost, IONOS, and Cloudways.

  • • Schema Validation: Every update checks that introductory rate, billing term length, renewal rate, and money-back guarantee fields pass strict bounds validation before touching data/hosting-deals.json.
  • • Fallback Preservation: If an upstream provider temporarily blocks automated requests or returns an incomplete response, SkyeFlare preserves the last verified baseline rather than displaying broken prices.
  • • Zero Fake Codes: We explicitly separate verified alphanumeric codes (such as Namecheap's monthly promo hub) from auto-applied checkout offers.

02. Editorial Independence & Ranking Methodology

Our Smart Filter & Recommendation Engine ranks matching providers mathematically by 36-month total cost (introductory term + subsequent renewal months + standard domain renewal after Year 1), resource fit, and renewal stability. Providers cannot pay to override TCO calculations or conceal their renewal rates.

03. Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

SkyeFlare remains free to use because it is supported by two transparent revenue streams:

  • • Affiliate Links: Outbound "Activate Deal" links may earn SkyeFlare a referral commission from the hosting provider at zero additional cost to you.
  • • Programmatic Advertising: We display clearly labeled, non-intrusive display advertisements through vetted ad partners (such as RevBid). Ad placements never cover interactive buttons or influence comparison rankings.
