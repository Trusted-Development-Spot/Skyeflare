Privacy Policy

SkyeFlare (skyeflare.com) respects your privacy. This policy explains what limited data is processed when you browse our hosting comparisons, use our cost calculators, or interact with advertisements and partner links.

1. Information We Collect

SkyeFlare is a static informational web application and does not require user accounts, logins, or payment information. When you contact us via email (contact@skyeflare.com), we process only the name, email address, and message content you voluntarily send in order to respond to your inquiry.

3. Third-Party Advertising Partners (Including RevBid)

To keep our comparison tools free, SkyeFlare partners with programmatic advertising networks, including RevBid and affiliated demand partners. Where permitted by your consent preferences, third-party ad networks may use cookies, web beacons, or device identifiers to deliver contextual or interest-based advertisements, measure ad impressions, and prevent invalid click fraud.

Users in the EEA, UK, or California may decline non-essential advertising cookies via our consent banner or opt out of personalized advertising through industry tools such as the NAI (optout.networkadvertising.org) or YourOnlineChoices.

4. Affiliate & Outbound Partner Links

When you click an "Activate Deal" link to visit Hostinger, Bluehost, Namecheap, SiteGround, A2 Hosting, DreamHost, IONOS, or Cloudways, the destination hosting provider or affiliate network may place a referral tracking cookie to attribute commissions. Once you leave skyeflare.com, the privacy policy of that third-party provider governs any data collected on their site.

5. Analytics & Hosting Infrastructure Logs

Our static hosting infrastructure may collect standard, aggregated HTTP access logs (such as IP address, user-agent, and requested URL) strictly for DDoS protection, uptime monitoring, and aggregate traffic measurement.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA/CPRA

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you have shared with us, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise these rights, email contact@skyeflare.com.
